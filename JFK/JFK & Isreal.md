# *Last President to stand up against Israel*



**Isreali nuclear program 1963**

The prime minister of Israel levi Eshkol asserted that, without nuclear weapons, Israel would not be able to survive. President John F. Kennedy repeatedly requested that neutral, international scientists be allowed to inspect the Israeli reactor twice a year, expressing grave concern over Israel's nuclear intentions.

These requests were pursued through CIA Director John McCone, and eventually access was granted. However, according to accounts from that period, Israel prepared for the inspections by constructing fake control rooms with fake control panels. Under the terms arranged for the visit, the inspectors were reportedly never permitted to examine the actual reactor core itself. After this visit, Kennedy is described as having remarked, “Those sons of bitches lie to me constantly about their nuclear capability.”

**Dallas**

Subsequently, Kennedy was invited to Dallas by local leaders. The visit was supported by the Dallas Citizens Council, which was directed by Julius Schepps, a member of the local chapter of the Anti-Defamation League’s B’nai B’rith organization.

Another confirmed member of B’nai B’rith, Abraham Zapruder, happened to be the person who filmed the assassination of Kennedy. Zapruder was a clothing manufacturer whose company was headquartered in the Dal-Tex Building, part of the Dallas textile district. According to certain ballistic analyses, it was from that building that the first two shots were allegedly fired and missed.

The building itself was owned by David Weisblat, who is described as one of the major financial supporters of the Anti-Defamation League, and Douglas Jaffe, noted as a significant donor to Lyndon B. Johnson.

The host committee that invited Kennedy to the Dallas event was chaired by Sam Bloom. According to statements attributed to the Dallas police, Bloom was one of the strongest advocates for transferring Lee Harvey Oswald from the Dallas Police Department to the Dallas County Jail.

It was during that transfer that Jack Ruby shot Oswald. Ruby’s birth name was Jacob Rubenstein.

Following Kennedy’s death, the Israeli reactor reportedly went critical, and Israel is widely believed to have obtained a nuclear weapon within a year or two thereafter.
